A verifiable Chinese-language data marketplace

Endangered-language preservation, as a self-policing market.

Miners ship ASR / TTS / MT models for Hokkien (v1 wedge), Cantonese, Tibetan, Uyghur, Wu… Validators score them with a 3-signal composite (native-speaker Elo + LLM back-translation + held-out FLORES-200) that survives weight-copying, Sybil speakers, and validator cabals. Buyers (Mozilla, 国家语委, UNESCO, iFlytek) read the on-chain weights and pay for vetted corpora.

Mechanism

A 3-signal composite. Every signal is independent — collusion would have to corrupt all three at once.

score(miner)

Weights are illustrative; the validator's actual coefficients live in scoring.py.

      + 0.3·FLORES   // rotated held-out professional corpus

Data & stats

Three charts that pin down what the subnet's three claims actually mean numerically — pulled from real runs of validator.py and attack.py in this repo. Every number is reproducible: re-run bash demo.sh and read the same outputs.

① Composite-score discrimination (验证力)

Three miners, three quality tiers, three independent signals — stacked. Each bar = composite weight share. Each colour band = one signal's contribution (0.4·Elo + 0.3·BLEU_bt + 0.3·FLORES). Source: python -m languageark.validator --eval-set hokkien.

Read: Miner A wins on all three signals — large bar, all bands present. Miner B has near-perfect BLEU (gold round-trip) but ~zero FLORES → the third signal flags the miner is memorising back-translation, not translating. C fails on Elo + FLORES; the signals don't all see the same things, which is exactly the anti-collusion property we wanted.

② Commit-reveal knockout (博弈力)

Weight-copying freeloader's per-tempo vTrust over 12 epochs. Same adversary, two networks. Source: python -m languageark.attack.

Read: Without commit-reveal, the parasite reads honest validators' weights in real time and copies — flat at 100 % vTrust. Turn commit-reveal on with period = 5: every weight the parasite can see is now stale by ~6 hours, and miner quality has drifted in the meantime, so its copied vector is wrong. Mean drops to ~42 %. One hyperparameter, 58-point swing.

③ The Sinitic-language data gap (产品力)

Open-corpus validated audio hours, log scale. Why "no Hokkien data" is overstated but "not enough Hokkien data" is the market opportunity. Sources: Common Voice corpus stats + TAT-ASR card + FormoSpeech HF + back-of-envelope for SG/MY/PH diaspora (≈ 0).

Read: Mandarin and Taiwanese Hokkien (TAT) are in roughly the same order of magnitude. Cantonese and the rest are 2-3 orders of magnitude smaller — and diaspora Hokkien, Wu, Xiang have effectively no open corpus at all. Closing those red bars is the v1 → v2 product roadmap.

Genuinely new (the 创意 part)

🪪

Stake-bonded speaker DAO

No existing subnet has an on-chain stake-and-attestation registry for human evaluators. Our Solidity contract is the org / anti-Sybil novelty.

🧮

3-signal composite

SN37 uses single-signal eval; SN1 uses single-signal LLM judge. We compose Elo + back-translation + held-out FLORES so fooling two requires fooling the third independently.

🧩

Cross-script Hokkien framing

SeamlessM4T-v2 has no __nan__; FLORES has only Latin-script nan_Latn. Building a real Han-script Hokkien corpus IS the deliverable.
